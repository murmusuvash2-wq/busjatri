FAQ

How many buses pass through Digha?

287 bus services stop at Digha. Check the departure board above for timings at this stop.

What is the first bus from Digha?

The earliest listed service at Digha is 2:50 AM (PRATIDIN) towards Arambagh.

What is the last bus from Digha?

The last listed service at Digha is 10:30 PM (LAXMI NARAYAN) towards Digha.

Which places can I reach from Digha by bus?

Direct buses from this stop head to Medinipur, Kolkata, Garia, Barasat, Asansol, Esplanade, Jhargram, Bishnupur.

Is there a night bus at Digha?

Yes — services are listed at Digha between 10 PM and 5 AM.
