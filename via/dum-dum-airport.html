FAQ

How many buses pass through Dum Dum Airport?

17 bus services stop at Dum Dum Airport. Check the departure board above for timings at this stop.

What is the first bus from Dum Dum Airport?

The earliest listed service at Dum Dum Airport is 7:40 AM (RAJDHANI EXPRESS) towards Ramjibanpur.

What is the last bus from Dum Dum Airport?

The last listed service at Dum Dum Airport is 6:35 PM (RAJDHANI EXPRESS) towards Karunamoyee.

Which places can I reach from Dum Dum Airport by bus?

Direct buses from this stop head to Karunamoyee, Solpatta, Kolkata (Esplanade), Goaltore, Barasat, Hijli Sorif, Manbazar, Sonamukhi.

Is there a night bus at Dum Dum Airport?

No night service is currently listed at Dum Dum Airport in the 10 PM – 5 AM window.
