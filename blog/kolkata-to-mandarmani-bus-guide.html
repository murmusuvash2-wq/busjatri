Kolkata to Mandarmani Bus Guide — Timings, Route and Weekend Tips

2026-09-28 · BusJatri Team

Mandarmani is the beach Kolkata runs to. On Friday evenings, thousands of tired office-goers pack their bags, and by Saturday morning the village by the sea in Purba Medinipur is a different place — full of Kolkatans looking for fish fry, waves and a long, empty stretch of sand to walk on. It is often called one of the longest motorable beaches in India, and the drive-in stretch along the casuarina-lined coast is something Digha cannot offer.

If you are planning a weekend trip from Kolkata, this guide covers everything you need — bus timings from our timetable database, the best route options, when to go, what to eat, and how to plan one perfect day by the sea.

Why Mandarmani Works for a Weekend

Unlike Digha, which has become crowded and commercial, Mandarmani still has long, quiet stretches where you can actually hear the waves. The beach is wide and flat, the water is safe for a decent distance, and red crabs come out in thousands at dawn — an early morning walk here is the single best thing about the trip.

It is close enough that you do not need to plan a long holiday. Leave Kolkata early on Saturday, reach by mid-morning, spend a full day and a night, and take a bus back on Sunday evening. That is the standard Mandarmani weekend, and it works.

Kolkata to Mandarmani by Bus: The Timings That Matter

There are two ways to do this journey by bus — a direct bus, or a bus to Digha or Contai followed by a short local ride. Here is what our timetable data shows for direct services:

  • Santosh Bus Service (AC) — departs Kolkata at 6:00 AM, direct to Mandarmani. This is the most convenient morning option — you reach the beach before lunch.
  • From nearby towns there are morning connections too: Habra to Mandarmani at 5:10 AM and Barasat to Mandarmani at 5:55 AM (both Santosh Bus Service, AC).

For the return journey, direct buses from Mandarmani to Kolkata run through the day, so you are not stuck waiting for one evening coach:

  • Mandarmani to Kolkata: 9:25 AM, 10:30 AM, 11:30 AM, 12:40 PM, 2:10 PM and 5:35 PM
  • Mandarmani to Salt Lake: 5:15 AM (good if you must reach the city early)

Fares: AC coaches on this route generally cost more than the non-AC government buses, but the comfort is worth it for a four-to-five hour ride. Confirm the exact fare when you book — it changes with fuel prices and season.

The Smarter Route: Bus to Digha, Then Across

Many regulars take a bus to Digha and then hop across to Mandarmani. Why? Because Kolkata to Digha has far more buses through the day — our data shows direct services leaving from 4:30 in the morning to late at night, including 6:00 AM, 6:30 AM and 8:00 AM departures — and from Digha you can get a shared taxi or local vehicle to Mandarmani in roughly 30 to 45 minutes.

This is also the best fallback if the direct Mandarmani bus is full on a weekend. Buy a Digha ticket, enjoy the familiar sea-road, and cross over.

If you prefer trains: there is no station at Mandarmani itself. The practical rail option is a train from Howrah towards Kanthi (Contai), and then a road leg of about 14 km to Mandarmani. Buses remain the simplest door-to-beach option.

Which Route Does the Bus Take?

Most buses from Kolkata head down the Contai (Kanthi) direction on the coastal route, crossing Kolaghat on the way and turning towards the sea just before Contai. The last stretch into Mandarmani — through Chaulkhola and the village roads — is where the sea air first comes in through the window. The journey usually takes four to five hours depending on traffic and the season, so a 6:00 AM start from Kolkata means toes in the sand by around 11 AM.

Best Time to Visit

  • October to February is the sweet spot — pleasant days, cool evenings, perfect for the beach.
  • March to June is hot and humid; if you go, plan indoor afternoons and beach time at dawn and dusk.
  • Monsoon (July to September) is risky — the coastal roads occasionally get waterlogged and buses slow down. If you must travel, keep half a day of buffer.
  • Weekends and holidays are crowded everywhere in Mandarmani — book your bus seat and hotel at least a few days ahead. On long weekends, the direct AC bus fills up first.

One Perfect Day in Mandarmani

5:45 AM — Sunrise walk. Walk out to the beach before six. The red crabs come out as the tide pulls back, and the low morning light on the wet sand is the best photography you will get all trip. Carry nothing valuable — just a camera phone and slippers.

8:00 AM — Breakfast. Hotels serve standard Bengali breakfasts; the local specialities are luchis with alur dom, and sweet tea. Order extra — you will be hungry after the beach walk.

9:30 AM — Beach time and water sports. The beach shacks offer banana boat rides and jet skis through the day. The sea is gentle for most of the year, but always ask the local operators about the day's tide before swimming far.

1:00 PM — Lunch. This is the main event: fresh fish — bhetki, prawns and pomfret, usually available at the smaller hotels and stalls if you ask a day ahead. Rice, dal, bhaja and a whole fried fish is the classic Mandarmani meal.

4:00 PM — The casuarina drive or village walk. Walk the tree-lined stretch behind the beach, or drive along the motorable sand at low tide (ask a local driver which stretch is safe).

6:00 PM — Sunset and evening chai. The beach quiets down after the day-trippers leave. Sit, drink tea, watch the fishing boats come in.

Food You Should Not Miss

Beyond the standard hotel menus, look for: fresh pomfret and prawn malai curry from local kitchens, fried fish at the beach shacks in the evening, and coconut water straight from the cart. If you are returning on Sunday, pick up packets of dried fish (shutki) sold near the beach road — a Purba Medinipur speciality.

Practical Tips

  1. Book the 6:00 AM direct bus for Saturday — it is the only comfortable morning departure and fills up by Thursday on peak weekends.
  2. Carry cash. Network coverage exists but small stalls and taxis prefer cash.
  3. Pack light and carry slippers — the last walk from the drop point to your hotel is on sand.
  4. Keep the return timings in mind — the 5:35 PM bus is the last direct one to Kolkata; miss it and your fallback is via Digha.
  5. Check the weather in monsoon — coastal roads can flood; buses run but slowly.

Frequently Asked Questions

How far is Mandarmani from Kolkata?

Roughly 160-170 km by road, depending on the route taken. By bus it takes about four to five hours.

Is there a direct bus from Kolkata to Mandarmani?

Yes — a direct AC bus leaves Kolkata at 6:00 AM, and several direct buses return from Mandarmani through the day. See our Kolkata to Mandarmani timetable page for the updated list.

Which is better for Mandarmani — bus or train?

For a weekend trip, the bus is simpler — it takes you close to the beach directly, while trains require a road leg of about 14 km from Kanthi station.

Is Mandarmani good for a one-day trip?

It is possible with the 6:00 AM bus, but you would see mostly the daytime beach. An overnight stay lets you catch the sunrise crabs — the best part of Mandarmani.

Mandarmani or Digha?

Digha is bigger, busier and has more trains and buses. Mandarmani is quieter, has a longer motorable beach and better fresh fish. Many people do Digha first, then move to Mandarmani for the night — the two are less than an hour apart by road.
