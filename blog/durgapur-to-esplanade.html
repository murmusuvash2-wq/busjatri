Durgapur to Esplanade Bus Time Table (25 Buses) — Timings, Route & Travel Guide

2026-09-21 · BusJatri Team

Not every bus route needs a holiday to justify itself. Durgapur to Esplanade is very much a working-day route — shopkeepers, students, families and office-goers fill these buses all week, which is exactly why 25 services run every day between 2:00 AM and 6:30 PM.

That last point matters more than it sounds. Because the service spreads across the day, you are never really stranded on this route — miss one bus and there is usually another along within a reasonable wait. The first departure leaves Durgapur at 2:00 AM, and the last of the day rolls out at 6:30 PM, which tells you something about how much life this road carries.

The road between Durgapur and Esplanade

Between boarding and arrival, the bus passes through:

  • Durgapur City Center
  • Dvc
  • Muchipara
  • Panagarh Bypass
  • Barddhaman Bypass
  • Shaktigarh
  • Dankuni
  • Salap

Each of these halts is a small world of its own — vendors hop on at some, school crowds at others — and knowing which stops come before yours makes the journey feel shorter. If you are new to the route, keep the list handy; the conductor will usually call out the bigger halts.

Who runs these buses?

Services on this route are run by Shyamoli Paribahan (11), Greenline (6), Express Line (5), WBTC (2), Royal Cruiser (1). In practice this matters less on the timetable and more on the day: government corporation buses follow depot schedules fairly strictly, while private services are often a little more flexible about halts. Either way, all of them get you there.

Small things worth knowing

  • If your day is tight, aim for one of the earlier departures — you give yourself a buffer for delays and still reach with daylight to spare.
  • Window seats on this side of the route get the better views; the front rows are quieter if you want to doze off.
  • Travelling with luggage? The boot fills up on festival days and weekends — the earliest bus is usually the roomiest.
  • Reach the stand about fifteen minutes before departure — seats on the popular runs fill up quickly, and standing for a long stretch is no fun.
