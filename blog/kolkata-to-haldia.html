Kolkata to Haldia Bus Time Table (28 Buses) — Timings, Route & Travel Guide

2026-09-24 · BusJatri Team

Some routes you simply learn by heart. For anyone shuttling between Kolkata and Haldia, the 5:30 AM departure is less a timetable entry and more a small ritual — the same familiar faces, the same tea at the stand, the same race for a window seat.

That last point matters more than it sounds. Because the service spreads across the day, you are never really stranded on this route — miss one bus and there is usually another along within a reasonable wait. The first departure leaves Kolkata at 5:30 AM, and the last of the day rolls out at 6:45 PM, which tells you something about how much life this road carries.

The road between Kolkata and Haldia

Along the way, the bus calls at the places that stitch this route together:

  • Kolaghat
  • Mecheda

Each of these halts is a small world of its own — vendors hop on at some, school crowds at others — and knowing which stops come before yours makes the journey feel shorter. If you are new to the route, keep the list handy; the conductor will usually call out the bigger halts.

A look at who plies the route:

Services on this route are run by SBSTC (28). In practice this matters less on the timetable and more on the day: government corporation buses follow depot schedules fairly strictly, while private services are often a little more flexible about halts. Either way, all of them get you there.

Small things worth knowing

  • Travelling with luggage? The boot fills up on festival days and weekends — the earliest bus is usually the roomiest.
  • Reach the stand about fifteen minutes before departure — seats on the popular runs fill up quickly, and standing for a long stretch is no fun.
  • Timings here are the listed schedule; seasonal traffic, breakdowns and market-day crowds can stretch them. A quick confirmation at the stand never hurts.
  • Keep some small change handy for the fare. Many private buses still run on exact change and goodwill.
