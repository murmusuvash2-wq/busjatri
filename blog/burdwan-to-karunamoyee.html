Burdwan to Karunamoyee Bus Time Table (21 Buses) — Timings, Route & Travel Guide

2026-09-22 · BusJatri Team

Ask anyone at the Burdwan bus stand how to reach Karunamoyee and you'll get a confident answer: just catch a bus. And they'd be right — with 21 services listed across the day, starting from 1:05 AM, this is one of those routes you can decide to travel on almost without planning.

That last point matters more than it sounds. Because the service spreads across the day, you are never really stranded on this route — miss one bus and there is usually another along within a reasonable wait. The first departure leaves Burdwan at 1:05 AM, and the last of the day rolls out at 6:30 PM, which tells you something about how much life this road carries.

The operators on this stretch:

Services on this route are run by SBSTC (21). In practice this matters less on the timetable and more on the day: government corporation buses follow depot schedules fairly strictly, while private services are often a little more flexible about halts. Either way, all of them get you there.

Small things worth knowing

  • Timings here are the listed schedule; seasonal traffic, breakdowns and market-day crowds can stretch them. A quick confirmation at the stand never hurts.
  • Keep some small change handy for the fare. Many private buses still run on exact change and goodwill.
  • If your day is tight, aim for one of the earlier departures — you give yourself a buffer for delays and still reach with daylight to spare.
  • Window seats on this side of the route get the better views; the front rows are quieter if you want to doze off.
