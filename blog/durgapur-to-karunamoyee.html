Durgapur to Karunamoyee Bus Time Table (30 Buses) — Timings, Route & Travel Guide

2026-09-19 · BusJatri Team

Ask anyone at the Durgapur bus stand how to reach Karunamoyee and you'll get a confident answer: just catch a bus. And they'd be right — with 30 services listed across the day, starting from 5:05 AM, this is one of those routes you can decide to travel on almost without planning.

That last point matters more than it sounds. Because the service spreads across the day, you are never really stranded on this route — miss one bus and there is usually another along within a reasonable wait. The first departure leaves Durgapur at 5:05 AM, and the last of the day rolls out at 5:50 PM, which tells you something about how much life this road carries.

The road between Durgapur and Karunamoyee

Between boarding and arrival, the bus passes through:

  • Durgapur City Center
  • Dvc
  • Muchipara
  • Panagarh
  • Barddhaman Bypass
  • Shaktigarh
  • Durgapur Expressway
  • Dankuni

Each of these halts is a small world of its own — vendors hop on at some, school crowds at others — and knowing which stops come before yours makes the journey feel shorter. If you are new to the route, keep the list handy; the conductor will usually call out the bigger halts.

A look at who plies the route:

Services on this route are run by Shyamoli Paribahan (11), Greenline (8), SBSTC (5), Express Line (5), WBTC (1). In practice this matters less on the timetable and more on the day: government corporation buses follow depot schedules fairly strictly, while private services are often a little more flexible about halts. Either way, all of them get you there.

Small things worth knowing

  • Travelling with luggage? The boot fills up on festival days and weekends — the earliest bus is usually the roomiest.
  • Reach the stand about fifteen minutes before departure — seats on the popular runs fill up quickly, and standing for a long stretch is no fun.
  • Timings here are the listed schedule; seasonal traffic, breakdowns and market-day crowds can stretch them. A quick confirmation at the stand never hurts.
  • Keep some small change handy for the fare. Many private buses still run on exact change and goodwill.
