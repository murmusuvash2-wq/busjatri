Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 September 2026

1. Overview
BusJatri (busjatri.in) is a free bus timetable website for West Bengal, India. This policy explains, in detail, what information we collect, how cookies and similar technologies are used (including Google Analytics and Google AdSense), which third parties are involved, and what rights you have over your data. We do not run user accounts, so we hold very little personal data — you can browse every timetable without a login and without telling us who you are.
2. Information we collect
a) Information you give us voluntarily. The contact form on our Contact page does not send data to our server — it opens your own email app with the message pre-filled, and you choose whether to send it. If you email us (for example a timing correction), we use your email address and message only to reply to you and, where relevant, to correct timetable data.
b) Information collected automatically. Like most websites, our hosting provider and the analytics/advertising services described below may automatically receive standard technical information when you visit: pages viewed, time of visit, browser and device type, language, referring page, and an approximate location derived from your IP address (usually city or region level). This is used to keep the site working, understand usage and (where shown) to display advertising.
c) Local preferences stored on your device. We use your browser's localStorage for two small preferences: bj-theme (your dark/light theme choice) and bj-lang (your English / বাংলা language choice). These never leave your device and are removed when you clear your browser data.
3. Cookies and similar technologies
A cookie is a small text file stored by your browser; localStorage works in a similar way but is only readable by this website. Here is the complete list of what this site sets:
• bj-theme (localStorage) — your theme choice; never expires on its own
• bj-lang (localStorage) — your language choice; never expires on its own
• _ga / _ga_* (cookies) — set by Google Analytics, used to distinguish visitors; they typically last up to 2 years
• Advertising cookies — if advertising is shown, Google and its certified partners may set additional cookies (including the DoubleClick cookie) to serve and measure ads
You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings (usually under "Privacy" or "Site settings"). Blocking cookies does not break this website — bus timetables remain fully usable; only personalised ads and analytics would stop working.
4. Google Analytics
We use Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID G-L4E3D1YX9X) to understand which pages are useful — for example, which routes people search for — so we can improve the timetables. Google Analytics collects data such as pages viewed, session duration, approximate location from IP address (city/region level), device and browser information, and referral source. This data is processed by Google LLC under its own Privacy Policy.
You can opt out of Google Analytics with the official Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on, or by blocking third-party cookies.
5. Advertising — Google AdSense
To keep BusJatri free, we may display advertisements served by Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies (including the DoubleClick DART cookie) to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.
• Opt out of personalised advertising: Google Ads Settings
• Opt out of third-party vendor cookies: aboutads.info/choices (or youronlinechoices.eu in the EU)
• How Google uses data from partner sites: policies.google.com — partner sites
• Google's advertising policies: policies.google.com/technologies/ads
6. Third-party services used by this site
• Google Fonts — loads the fonts used on this site; Google may log requests (IP address, requested font).
• Google Analytics / Google AdSense — see sections 4 and 5 above.
• Open-Meteo — destination weather on bus detail pages; queries contain a place name only.
• Nominatim / OpenStreetMap — optional location detection (see section 7).
• Cloudflare — our hosting/CDN provider; processes standard request data (IP address, timing) to serve pages and protect against abuse.
7. Geolocation (optional)
The search box can optionally detect your city to pre-fill the "From" field. Your browser asks for permission first. If you allow it, your coordinates are sent once to Nominatim (OpenStreetMap) to resolve a city name, and we never store them. If you deny permission, nothing happens and the site works normally.
8. What we do NOT do
We do not sell, rent or trade your personal data. We do not run user accounts or user profiles. We do not send marketing emails. We do not require any personal information to use the timetables.
9. Data retention
Local preferences (bj-theme, bj-lang) stay on your device until you clear them. Emails you send us are deleted once your query is resolved. Analytics data is retained by Google according to its own policies (the default Google Analytics retention setting is 14 months of activity data).
10. Children
BusJatri is a general-audience bus timetable and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children.
11. Your rights
Because we do not maintain user accounts, there is normally no personal data for us to export or delete. If you have emailed us and want your message deleted, or if you have any privacy question or complaint, write to busjatri@zohomail.in and we will respond as soon as possible. Depending on where you live, you may also have rights under data-protection law (such as India's DPDP Act or, for visitors from Europe, the GDPR) to access, correct or delete personal data — the same email address works for all such requests.
12. Do Not Track
This site does not respond to browser "Do Not Track" signals in a special way, but you can control tracking cookies through your browser settings and the opt-out links in sections 4 and 5.
13. Changes to this policy
If we change this policy, the "Last updated" date above will change. Material changes will be highlighted on this page.
14. Contact
Questions about privacy? Email busjatri@zohomail.in.
