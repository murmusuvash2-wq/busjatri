How many buses run from Habra to Digha via Kolkata Airport?

3 of the 13 buses on the Habra to Digha route pass through Kolkata Airport.

What is the first bus from Habra to Digha via Kolkata Airport?

The first bus passing through Kolkata Airport departs Habra at 5:00 AM.

What is the last bus from Habra to Digha via Kolkata Airport?

The last bus passing through Kolkata Airport departs Habra at 5:30 AM.

Do I have to change buses at Kolkata Airport?

No, these are through-buses. They pass through Kolkata Airport on the way from Habra to Digha.

When do buses reach Kolkata Airport from Habra?

Buses reach Kolkata Airport between 5:52 AM and 6:28 AM depending on departure time.
