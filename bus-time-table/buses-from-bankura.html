What is the first bus from Bankura Bus Stand?

The first listed departure from Bankura Bus Stand is at 3:40 AM — RAJDHANI, to Kolkata (Esplanade). Buses often leave once seats fill up, so arrive a little early.

What is the last bus from Bankura Bus Stand?

The last listed departure is 6:00 PM — GURUNANAK, to Siliguri. More services may exist, so ask at the stand.

How many buses start from Bankura Bus Stand daily?

Which places can I go from Bankura Bus Stand by bus?

From Bankura Bus Stand you can travel to Durgapur, Esplanade, Tarkeshwar, Jhargram, Asansol, Nabadwip and others — the full destination list with bus counts is on this page.

Which operators run buses from Bankura Bus Stand?

Listed services include AAYAN TRAVELS, INDRA TRAVELS, ISHA TRAVELS, ISHIKA TRAVELS and others. Government (SBSTC/NBSTC/WBTC) and private buses both serve this stand.
