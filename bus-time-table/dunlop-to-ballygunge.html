FAQs (সাধারণ প্রশ্ন)

What time is the first and last S-9A bus?

First bus leaves Dunlop at 05:50 and Ballygunge at 04:20. Last bus leaves Dunlop at 21:25 and Ballygunge at 21:15.

How often does S-9A run?

Roughly every 15 minutes through the day — about 120 services daily across both directions.

Which metro stations are on the S-9A route?

Twelve Blue Line stations including Shyambazar, Girish Park, Central, Chandni Chowk, Esplanade, Park Street, Maidan, Rabindra Sadan and Kalighat. Esplanade is a Blue + Green interchange.

About route S-9A (রুট সম্পর্কে)

S-9A is a CSTC (government) city bus in Kolkata running between Dunlop in the north and Ballygunge in the south, via Shyambazar, Girish Park, Central, Esplanade, Park Street, Rabindra Sadan, Kalighat and Gariahat. It is one of the most metro-connected city routes — twelve Blue Line stations sit on or beside the route, and Esplanade connects to the Green Line. Landmarks along the way include Hatibagan, Writers' Building, the Victoria Memorial, Nandan, Kalighat Kali Temple and Gariahat market.
