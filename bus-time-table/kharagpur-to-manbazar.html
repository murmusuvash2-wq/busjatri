The bus route connecting Kharagpur and Manbazar is a major passenger corridor in West Bengal covering approximately 174.0 km via the direct West Bengal state highway corridor connecting Kharagpur and Manbazar. Passengers have a choice of both government transport corporations (such as SBSTC, Atithi) and regular private line buses.
