FAQ প্রশ্নোত্তর

What is the first bus from Digha Bus Stand?

The first listed departure from Digha Bus Stand is at 2:50 AM — PRATIDIN, to Arambagh. Buses often leave once seats fill up, so arrive a little early.

What is the last bus from Digha Bus Stand?

The last listed departure is 10:00 PM — SBSTC Digha-Kolkata, to Kolkata. More services may exist, so ask at the stand.

How many buses start from Digha Bus Stand daily?

149 buses are listed starting from Digha Bus Stand, serving 49 destinations. More services may exist — ask at the stand.

Which places can I go from Digha Bus Stand by bus?

From Digha Bus Stand you can travel to Esplanade, Barasat, Habra, Medinipur, Baruipur, Belgharia and others — the full destination list with bus counts is on this page.

Which operators run buses from Digha Bus Stand?

Listed services include ANUSKA TRAVELS, GANGARAM TRAVEL'S, MAA USHA TRAVELS, Maa Kajal Travels and others. Government (SBSTC/NBSTC/WBTC) and private buses both serve this stand.
