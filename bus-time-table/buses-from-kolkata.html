What is the first bus from Kolkata (Esplanade)?

The first listed departure from Kolkata (Esplanade) is at 4:30 AM — NOOR TRAVELS, to Bishnupur. Buses often leave once seats fill up, so arrive a little early.

What is the last bus from Kolkata (Esplanade)?

The last listed departure is 11:30 PM — SBSTC Kolkata-Digha, to Digha. More services may exist, so ask at the stand.

How many buses start from Kolkata (Esplanade) daily?

383 buses are listed starting from Kolkata (Esplanade), serving 190 destinations. More services may exist — ask at the stand.

Which places can I go from Kolkata (Esplanade) by bus?

From Kolkata (Esplanade) you can travel to Haldia, Durgapur, Asansol, Burdwan, Midnapur, Digha and others — the full destination list with bus counts is on this page.

Which operators run buses from Kolkata (Esplanade)?

Listed services include Mahamaya, Mini Bus, NBSTC, Private and others. Government (SBSTC/NBSTC/WBTC) and private buses both serve this stand.
