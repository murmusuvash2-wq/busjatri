What is the last bus from Haldia to Bankura?
The last departure listed is at 8:50 PM — MADAN MOHAN. No later direct bus is listed on BusJatri for the same day, so plan accordingly.
How long does the Haldia to Bankura bus journey take?
Most listed buses cover this route in about 7h 05m. The fastest service takes about 6h 40m, while slower ones can take up to 7h 30m. Actual time depends on stops, traffic and the season.
