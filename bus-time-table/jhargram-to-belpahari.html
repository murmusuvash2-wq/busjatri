What is the first bus from Jhargram to Belpahari?

What is the last bus from Jhargram to Belpahari?
The last departure listed is at 6:30 PM — JAI MAA DURGA. No later direct bus is listed on BusJatri for the same day, so plan accordingly.
How long does the Jhargram to Belpahari bus journey take?
Most listed buses cover this route in about 1h 15m. The fastest service takes about 55m, while slower ones can take up to 1h 30m. Actual time depends on stops, traffic and the season.
Which operators run buses from Jhargram to Belpahari?
Services listed on this route include AMARNATH TRAVELS, BABA BASANTA ROY TRAVELS, KALYAN TRAVEL'S, Kalyan Travels — roughly 7 government services and 27 private services. AC coaches are also listed on this route.
How many buses run from Jhargram to Belpahari?
34 buses are listed for this route on BusJatri, spread from 5:00 AM to 6:30 PM — 14 in the morning and 20 later in the day. More services may exist beyond what is listed.
