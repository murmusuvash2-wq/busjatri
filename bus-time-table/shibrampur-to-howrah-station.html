FAQs (সাধারণ প্রশ্ন)

Does 12C/1 have a timetable?

No. Private city buses like 12C/1 have no published schedule — they run at short intervals through the day. Only government routes (CSTC/WBTC) publish departure times.

Which metro stations are on the 12C/1 route?

Five: Rabindra Sadan, Maidan and Park Street on the Blue Line, Esplanade (Blue + Green interchange) and Howrah on the Green Line.

About route 12C/1 (রুট সম্পর্কে)

12C/1 is a private, non-AC city bus in Kolkata. It links Shibrampur in the Behala area of south-west Kolkata with Howrah Station, running through Behala Chowrasta, Kidderpore, Hastings, Rabindra Sadan, Park Street, Esplanade and BBD Bag. The route touches five metro stations and passes landmarks such as the Victoria Memorial, Nandan, Writers' Building and Howrah rail terminal. Like all private city buses, 12C/1 has no published timetable; buses run at short intervals through the day.
