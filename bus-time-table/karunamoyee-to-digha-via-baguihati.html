FAQসাধারণ প্রশ্ন (FAQ)

How many buses run from Karunamoyee to Digha via Baguihati?

2 of the 3 buses on the Karunamoyee to Digha route pass through Baguihati.

What is the first bus from Karunamoyee to Digha via Baguihati?

The first bus passing through Baguihati departs Karunamoyee at 5:40 AM.

What is the last bus from Karunamoyee to Digha via Baguihati?

The last bus passing through Baguihati departs Karunamoyee at 12:45 PM.

Do I have to change buses at Baguihati?

No, these are through-buses. They pass through Baguihati on the way from Karunamoyee to Digha.

When do buses reach Baguihati from Karunamoyee?

Buses reach Baguihati between 6:14 AM and 1:19 PM depending on departure time.
